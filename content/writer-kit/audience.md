# SLP Transitions Audience

Primary reader:

An intelligent speech-language pathologist who is burned out, restless, curious about non-clinical work, or unsure whether the profession still fits.

They may:

- feel guilty about leaving a helping profession
- fear wasting their degree
- worry about salary or stability
- struggle to identify transferable skills
- have little exposure to business or tech roles
- feel overwhelmed by vague career advice
- want meaningful work without starting over completely

Assume:

- they understand clinical SLP language
- they do not need to be told that burnout is difficult
- they want honest tradeoffs rather than cheerleading
- they respond well to evidence, stories, and experiments
- they may be skeptical of career coaches promising transformation

Desired reaction:

> I feel seen, I learned something real, and I know what I can test next.
