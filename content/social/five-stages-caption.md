## Caption (paste as is)

Leaving speech pathology rarely happens in one decision. It happens in five stages.

1. Private doubt. You search at 11pm and clear the history.
2. Guilt and identity. The degree, the loans, the families who still text you updates.
3. Permission-seeking. You read every "I left, here's what I do" post to the end.
4. Practical panic. You've said it out loud, to a partner or to the steering wheel.
5. Action. You've applied to some things, and the silence is starting to feel personal.

Which one are you in? Tell me the number.
