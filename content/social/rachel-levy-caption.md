## Caption (paste as is)

Rachel Levy was an SLP for 16 years. Schools, private practice, mostly pediatrics.

She got a doctorate hoping it would springboard her somewhere new. Then she opened her own practice and knew that wasn't it either.

What moved things was a mentor asking one question: what do you want to be doing?

Rachel said tech. The mentor was close friends with a founder and told her to talk to him. After they talked, she was offered a job as customer success manager at Voiceitt.

Her answer was ready when someone asked.

What would you say?

Rachel's full story is at slptransitions.com (link in bio). This clip is from her interview with Mai Ling Chan on the Xceptional Leaders podcast.
