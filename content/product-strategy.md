# SLP Transitions — Product Suite Strategy

Synthesis of: Jenny Blake's Pivot method (operational detail), Daphne Gomez/Teacher Career Coach's business architecture, 2025-26 courses-vs-tools market data, James's Notion idea backlog, and the niche research already done.

## The one-line strategy

**Build the Gomez ladder, but with software where she used PDFs — because shipping AI tools is the one thing nobody else in this niche can do.**

## Why this shape (the evidence)

- **Career change is episodic** → one-time purchases and a product *ladder* fit; pure-tool subscriptions monetize thinly (Teal: 100k+ paying users but only ~5% convert, $4.2M revenue, unprofitable, now pivoting to coaching). Recurring revenue only works attached to an *active ongoing process* (community, cohorts, job-search tracking).
- **The proven ladder next door** (Teacher Career Coach): free 9-question quiz → **$9 guide converting ~24% of quiz takers (20k+ buyers, ~$100k/yr from the $9 product alone)** → $179 course → $99-119/listing job board → staffing company. The $9 product is the engine: it makes *customers*, not leads.
- **Courses aren't dead — info-dumps are.** Structured transformation still sells; cohort formats complete at ~64% vs ~48% self-paced. What AI killed is paying $599 for information. What survives: structure, accountability, personalization, tools.
- **B2B/licensing is the quiet margin layer** in every studied business (Blake's train-the-trainer + licensing, Gomez's job board + staffing arm). Comes later, after audience.

## The ladder (target state)

| Rung | Product | Price | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Career quiz (upgraded → "Pivot Compass" diagnostic) | Free | Quiz exists; upgrade it |
| 1 | **Personalized Pivot Report** | $9 | BUILD NEXT — the customer-maker |
| 2 | Career Pivot Suite (resume/materials tool) | $24 | ✅ Live |
| 3 | **Transition OS** — guided 90-day interactive program | $79-99 (includes #2) | Build after #1 proves funnel |
| 4 | Quarterly cohort sprint (30-day, community + weekly call + tools) | $149-199 | Only when email list supports it |
| 5 | Job board / employer layer via podcast network | B2B pricing | Later — monetizes audience, doesn't build it |

**Bundling:** à la carte + "everything" bundle at ~$99. Keep the brand promise everywhere: one-time, no subscription. (Subscription only ever for live community, where it's honest.)

### Rung 1: The $9 Personalized Pivot Report (build next)
Daphne's $9 PDF, but generated: the quiz feeds the existing AI pipeline and produces a personal readout — career operating mode, top 3 role matches with real salary/timeline data (from the verified research), phase diagnosis (where they are in the pivot cycle), and a 30-day starter plan. Impulse-priced, instantly delivered, upsells into the $24 tool with credit ("your $9 counts toward the full suite" — optional). All infrastructure already exists: Stripe, generation pipeline, email.

### Rung 3: Transition OS (the "interactive game," made practical)
A full game is overkill — but the *quest-log structure* is right. A guided 90-day web experience implementing the four-stage pivot pattern in original language (e.g., **Ground → Explore → Test → Leap**; do NOT ship Blake's "Plant/Scan/Pilot/Launch" naming or exercises verbatim — she actively licenses the method. The generic pattern is unprotectable; her language/worksheets are hers):
- **Ground:** values + strengths + one-year vision setup (feeds every later AI generation); riskometer-style self-check
- **Explore:** informational-interview missions with scripts + a contact tracker; role research cards from the verified research data
- **Test:** a pilot-experiment menu (volunteer projects, side gigs, proof artifacts…) with a debrief worksheet after each (enjoyed it? good at it? does it expand income/skills?)
- **Leap:** launch-criteria checklist (financial runway, milestone, deadline) + the full resume/materials suite for the chosen target
- Progress bar, phases, streaks — quest log, not literal game. An AI coach chat grounded in the research answers questions along the way.
This is the honest AI-age replacement for the $599 course, at $79-99, and it's software — the one format competitors can't copy.

1. **James ships software.** TNCPT and TCC sell PDFs and videos. Every rung here is a tool.
2. **Xceptional Leaders podcast = employer-side access.** Nobody else in the niche has a standing line to disability/health-tech founders and leaders — i.e., the people who *hire*. Uses: (a) end-of-interview question for every guest — "does your company hire former clinicians, and for what roles?" → enriches the companies DB with named, sourced entries; (b) "hiring in the disability space" episode segments cross-posted to SLP Transitions; (c) the eventual job-board/talent-collective seed (Daphne's staffing arm is the precedent); (d) instant guest-swap credibility for the SLP-podcast circuit.
3. **James is the proof point.** SLP → growth marketing in mental-health tech. The origin story is the marketing.

## Verdicts on the Notion backlog (the ADHD check)

- **Pivot Hexagon / Compass prompts / restructured explore mode** → YES, absorbed into Rungs 0-1-3 above (quiz upgrade + Ground phase). Prior instinct was right.
- **Course with custom GPT** → superseded; Transition OS *is* this idea, built as a tool instead of a course. Don't build a course.
- **30-day sprint community** → good idea, wrong time. It's Rung 4; needs an email list to fill cohorts. Park until the funnel produces ~1k engaged subscribers.
- **Job board** → premature. TCC added hers *after* 100k subscribers; boards monetize audiences, they don't create them. The "slp jobs" keyword data in the old note is for *clinical* jobs anyway — different intent. Park.
- **SLP Stash** (therapy-product directory + CEUs/ads) → **different business, different audience** (clinicians staying in the field vs. leaving it). It would split focus, brand, and SEO. Park it hard, or spin it off someday with its own name.
- **Alma-for-SLPs** → a marketplace startup, not a product rung. Park.
- **"Sunday scaries" newsletter name** → charming, still usable as the newsletter's nickname.

## Sequencing (the discipline)

1. **Now:** publish the 12 posts, load the email sequences, pitch Rehab Rebels. Distribution before new products — nothing on the ladder matters at zero traffic.
2. **Next build (small):** quiz upgrade + $9 report. One funnel: quiz → $9 report → $24 suite. Measure the 24% benchmark.
3. **Then:** Transition OS at $79-99, bundle at ~$99.
4. **Only after list growth:** cohort sprint, then employer layer.

One audience (SLPs leaving clinical), one funnel, one ladder. Everything that doesn't feed it waits.
