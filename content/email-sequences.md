### Email 5 — day 10
**Subject:** The uncomfortable math of job applications
**Preview text:** Referrals decide most transitions. Here's the playbook nobody follows.

Quick math from the transitions I've studied:

Mass-applying on Indeed: one SLP sent **500+ applications** over 6 months before landing her analyst role. It worked — but she'd tell you the volume wasn't the strategy, it was the tax she paid for not having referrals.

The pattern across almost every successful transition: **someone on the inside**. A former colleague. An SLP who left the year before. A stranger who answered a genuinely curious message.

The playbook, such as it is:
1. Find people doing your target job (LinkedIn search: "former SLP" + the role — there are more of us out there than you think).
2. Ask for 20 minutes. Be curious, not needy. Expect 20-25% to ignore you — normal, not personal.
3. Apply within 48 hours of a posting going live. Applications in the first 1-2 days get seen; day-9 applications mostly don't.

One more thing: your degree isn't a prison, and neither is your zip code — a lot of these roles are remote.

— James
