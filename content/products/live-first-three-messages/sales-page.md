## Who this is for

You've decided you want out, or you're close, and the next step everyone gives you is "network". You've stared at LinkedIn, typed half a message to someone who left, and closed the tab. This session is for that exact moment.

You don't need a polished résumé or a final job title. If you're torn between two paths, bring both. The free career quiz takes two minutes if you'd like a starting point before the call.

---

## Why messages, and why now

Most SLPs who land a non-clinical job get there through a person. One SLP in our research sent 113 tailored applications over eleven months for a single offer. The moves that go faster almost always have someone inside who could say, "I talked to her, she's serious."

You already know how to talk to strangers: you build rapport with a four-year-old, a nurse and a grandmother before lunch. What's missing is people who work somewhere else, and a first message to one of them that doesn't feel impossible to write. That message is what we'll write together.

---

## What happens in the 75 minutes

1. **Build your list (10 minutes).** Ten names you could message this week: your grad cohort, the reps who walk into your building, the clinicians who already left, the non-clinical teams at your own employer.
2. **Find them on LinkedIn (10 minutes).** The two searches that turn up former SLPs in the job you want, live on screen.
3. **Write the message (15 minutes).** The message that gets ignored next to the one that gets answered, and the five things that make the difference. You draft yours from a template built for SLPs.
4. **Send three (15 minutes).** A timer, quiet music, and your three messages going out. Volunteers can share a draft, and I'll edit it live.
5. **What happens next (10 minutes).** When to follow up, the two questions to ask when someone says yes, and how to keep the conversation going without being a pest.
6. **Your questions (15 minutes).**

## Questions

**What if I don't know what job I want yet?**
Bring two titles you're weighing. Messages to people in both are how you find out which one fits. If you have no idea, take the free career quiz first; it takes two minutes and names a path.

**Do I have to be on camera or share my messages?**
No. Camera is optional, and sharing a draft is only for volunteers.

**What if I can't make it live?**
Everyone who pays gets the replay and the templates. You'll get the most out of it live, because the sending happens on the call.

**I already bought the Getting Started kit. Is this the same thing?**
It uses the same templates, so you already have the words. What this adds is an hour where the messages actually get written and sent, with someone to edit them live. If that's not what you need, skip this one.

**What if not enough people sign up?**
If fewer than 15 people join by November 5, I'll cancel and refund everyone in full. You'll hear from me either way that day.

**Can I get a refund otherwise?**
Yes. Ask any time before the session and you'll get the full $29 back.

**Will people actually answer my messages?**
Some won't, and that's normal. Expect roughly one in four or five to stay quiet. People agree to direct requests far more often than askers predict, and many former SLPs remember sending these exact messages themselves. The session covers the follow-up for the ones who don't.
