# Transition OS: what it needs before it sells (2026-11-15)

James asked for the course to be made ready for production: the most useful and actionable version, with activities, a well-designed workbook and a good experience, plus a view on video and on a pre-order waitlist. This is the audit, what was fixed the same day, and the recommendation.

## What exists

The course is written. 48 lessons across eight modules, about 8.5 hours, with 20 interactive tools, a people list and an application tracker, a workbook that fills in from the reader's answers, a blank printable workbook in two editions, and two printable sheets. Progress syncs to the purchase. Module 0 is free and the $19 kit opens Module 1 and five later lessons.

| Module | Lessons | Minutes | Ends in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Start here (free) | 4 | 22 | Your hourly rate, income floor, a date, a verdict |
| 1 Ground | 7 | 39 | Your reasons in writing |
| 2 Explore | 7 | 54 | A path and the hours you can give it |
| 3 Connect | 8 | 82 | Three messages sent, two conversations kept warm |
| 4 Translate | 7 | 80 | A translated résumé and one application sent properly |
| 5 Test | 4 | 142 | One piece of proof, and your runway |
| 6 Leap | 8 | 65 | Interview answers, and an offer checked |
| 7 After | 3 | 29 | Your story, and the next rung |

## What it cannot do yet

Nobody can buy it. There is no checkout for the full program, no sales page and no price on the site. A visitor sees "Full program · Coming soon".

## Fixed on 2026-11-15

1. **Eleven lessons had nothing to do but read** (four in a row in Module 2). Each now ends with one written answer that saves as the reader types and prints in their workbook.
2. **The filled-in workbook stopped at Module 1.** It now has a page for each later module: hours and paths, the people list, numbers and the translated bullet, applications, the proof piece and runway, interview answers and the offer check, and the year-in page.
3. **The welcome lesson told buyers "these four lessons are free" and "after that, $19".** It now speaks to what the reader owns.
4. One resource said "260 companies that hire former SLPs". It says "that value clinical skills" now.

## Still missing, ranked

1. **Proof that people will pay.** The $19 kit has sold three times. A read-and-do course from a stranger is the hardest thing on this ladder to sell, so demand should be shown before anything else is built or recorded.
2. **A face and a voice.** There is no video and no photo of James inside the course. Every competitor leads with a person.
3. **Real people.** The lessons use short forum quotes and no named stories: the `story` block exists and is used nowhere. Five finished interviews sit unpublished.
4. **Something that brings people back each week.** No emails go to a program buyer after the receipt. Read-and-do courses are finished by people who are reminded.
5. **A place to ask.** There is no way to ask James a question from inside a lesson.
6. **Long lessons on a phone.** Lesson 3.1 runs 2,688 words against a spec of 500 to 900, and 3.2 is over seven screens long.
7. **A way to buy**: sales page, checkout with the kit's $19 credited, the access email, and a validated money path.

## Recommendation

**Pre-sell it before recording anything.** A free waitlist overstates interest, so take real pre-orders with a stated opening date and a full refund if the course does not open.

- **Offer:** founding price of $79 until it opens, $129 after. The operating plan had $99. Teacher Career Coach sells its course at $129 and Mass General Brigham's 12-week series was $99.
- **Gate:** the plan's own test. Go if 15 people join and 8 pay by a set date. If not, refund everyone and write down why.
- **Dates:** pre-orders open this month, decide on December 15, open on Monday January 11. January is when people decide to leave.
- **Video:** eight short videos, not thirty. One welcome and one opener per module, 60 to 90 seconds each, recorded in one afternoon after the gate passes. One two-minute video for the pre-order page is worth recording now.
- **Instead of live calls:** twelve weekly emails, one per week of the program, each naming that week's one action, with "reply to this and I'll answer" as the place to ask.
