### Why this quiz asks what it asks

Most career quizzes only ask what you enjoy. That's half the picture, and it's the half that gets people stuck chasing paths they can't realistically reach right now.

So this one also asks two things you won't see elsewhere:

**What you've actually done.** Whether you were the EMR superuser, whether you've supervised CFs, whether you've handled authorizations — these decide which doors are genuinely open. If you fixed the documentation system at your last job, clinical informatics roles pay $97,000–$154,000 and that experience is the qualification. Nobody tells SLPs that.

**What you actually need.** If you have to match your salary from day one, telling you to spend six months building an instructional-design portfolio is bad advice, however well it suits your personality. Your income floor and the time you realistically have change which recommendations are honest.

### Where SLPs actually end up

The quiz can land you on any of eight paths. These aren't aspirational — they're where documented transitions actually go:

| Path | Typical range | Typical timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Success / Implementation | $75,000–$120,000 | 3–9 months |
| Project / Program Management | $85,000–$100,000+ | 12–15 months |
| Healthcare Data / Analytics | $70,000–$105,000 | 6–24 months |
| Clinical Liaison / Utilization Review | $80,000–$135,000 | Often weeks |
| Clinical Informatics / EHR | $97,000–$154,000 | 6–18 months |
| Instructional Design / Learning | $70,000–$100,000 | 6–12 months |
| Content / Marketing | $80,000–$141,000 | ~12 months |
| Clinical Educator / Trainer | $75,000–$116,000 | Moderate |

A few things worth noticing in that table.

**Clinical liaison and utilization review are the fastest exits**, because your license *is* the qualification — no bootcamp, no portfolio, no certificate. If you need out sooner rather than later, start there.

**Project management is the most-traveled road**, with more documented six-figure outcomes than any other path. Your caseload has been concurrent project management this whole time; it just didn't come with the title.

**Nothing here takes six weeks.** The realistic range across every path is 6–15 months. One SLP I studied took 11 months, 113 tailored applications, and 7 interviews to get 1 offer. She'd tell you it was worth every week — but she'd also tell you nobody warned her.

### Frequently asked questions

About two minutes. Eight questions, no long-form writing.

Yes — the result is emailed to you so you don't lose it, and you'll also get the companies database. You can unsubscribe any time and I don't share your address with anyone.

**Is this actually free?**
Yes. There's a paid report ($9) if you want the version built on your actual resume, but the quiz and its result cost nothing.

**Will it tell me to quit my job?**
No. Some people take this and decide a different clinical setting is the real answer — that's a legitimate outcome, and the quiz will say so if your answers point there.

**What if I already know which role I want?**
