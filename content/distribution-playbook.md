# SLP Transitions — Distribution Playbook

Grounded in July 2026 channel research. Solo-founder time budget assumed: ~5-8 hrs/week on distribution. The strategy in one line: **own the thin long-tail SEO nobody defends, borrow audiences via podcasts, and turn the affiliate vacuum into a channel — while the honest-tool positioning does the selling.**

## The strategic picture

- **You are already ranking.** slptransitions.com appears in live SERPs for "leaving the SLP field," "alternative careers for speech language pathologists," "SLP burnout quitting," "SLP to UX," and "slp career change." This is not a cold start — SEO is your strongest existing asset.
- **One dominant competitor, wrong lane.** The Non-Clinical PT: 46k email subs, 51-55k FB group, $599 course — but PT-first (SLP is her third audience), weak on Instagram (~7k), and **no affiliate program**. The SLP-specific lane is underowned.
- **The playbook is proven next door.** Teacher Career Coach: 100k email subs in ~3 years via SEO + a 9-question quiz converting ~24% of takers into buyers of a low-priced product, plus a 30% affiliate program. Your $24 tool + existing quiz is structurally the same machine at SLP scale.

## Week-by-week: the first 90 days

### Weeks 1-2 — Publish + plumbing
- Publish posts #1-3 (resume, customer success, transferable skills) with the `slp-blog-aeo` skill treatment (question H2s, FAQ schema).
- Load the three email sequences into MailerLite; wire the "Customers" group trigger.
- Update the flagship "13 Non-Clinical Paths" post to link to each new role page (internal-link juice + it already ranks).
- Add the tool CTA to the site's top 3 ranking pages — a simple banner or inline mention on the flagship post alone puts the tool in front of your existing search traffic. **This is the single highest-leverage 30 minutes on this list.**

### Weeks 2-3 — Podcast blitz (borrowed audiences)
Pitch order, warmest first:
1. **Rehab Rebels** (rehabrebels.org/guests) — literally "OTs, PTs, and SLPs transition to alternative careers," has an open guest page. Easiest yes in the niche.
2. **SLP Happy Hour** — recurring burnout/"considered leaving?" segments.
3. **The SLP Now Podcast** — has run leaving-the-field stories.
4. **Fix SLP** — 40k IG, loudest disaffected-SLP audience; advocacy-focused so pitch the *data* angle ("what 500 documented SLP transitions actually look like"), not the product.
5. **ASHA Voices** — has done two nonclinical-careers episodes; high credibility, slower yes.

The pitch: your origin story (clinician → marketer) + the research ("I studied every documented SLP transition I could find — here's where people actually land and what it actually takes"). You're a data-bearing guest, not a seller. The tool gets one mention + a memorable URL.

### Weeks 3-6 — Publish the rest + Reddit presence
- Posts #4-8 on cadence (1-2/week).
- Reddit: become a genuine daily presence in **r/SLPcareertransitions** (small, exact-audience, low moderation friction) under your real identity. In **r/slp** (big, strict): answer "how do I get out" threads with substance — the translation table, the real timelines — links only when asked, tool in your profile. The rule: 9:1 value-to-promo. One good "I analyzed every documented SLP transition — AMA" post in the small sub after you've been present a few weeks.
- Each published post gets its "Reddit-ready value comment" version (the knowledge, no links) to deploy when relevant threads appear.

### Weeks 6-8 — Partnerships + affiliate
- **Launch a 30% affiliate program** (Stripe promotion codes or a simple affiliate tool). The niche benchmark is Teacher Career Coach's 30%; The Non-Clinical PT has NO program — you'd be the only tool in the niche that pays partners.
- First affiliate targets: SLP burnout/career coaches who don't sell a competing tool (Jessi Andricks / SLP Stress Management, The SLP Burnout Coach, Career Coach Jess). Their clients need exactly this; $7.20/sale isn't the point — it makes recommending you frictionless and official.
- **FB group admins**: pitch "SLPs Leaving the Field" (~9.8k members) a free live Q&A or member discount code — relationship first, never link-drop. One admin yes beats months of posting.
- **Newsletter swaps**: Kayla Hill's Substack (former SLP → SEO analyst, engaged audience) and adjacent micro-newsletters.

### Weeks 8-12 — Pinterest + repurposing engine
- Pinterest: the teacher-transition analog has auto-generated interest hubs and 130-pin boards; **the SLP version doesn't exist.** First-mover: 2-3 pins per blog post (list-format title cards), boards for "Non-Clinical SLP Jobs," "SLP Resume Tips," "Companies That Hire SLPs." Schedule ~1 hr/week.
- Instagram (optional): before/after resume-bullet carousels are the one format tied directly to the product. Or skip organic entirely and do shoutout/partnership posts with Fix SLP-adjacent creators.
- Each blog post also becomes: 1 newsletter edition, 1 pin, 1 carousel, 1 Reddit value comment. Write once, distribute four times.

## What spreads in this niche (make more of these)
1. **The quiz** — already on the site; wire its results page to the tool's free preview. (Teacher Career Coach converts ~24% of quiz takers to a paid report.)
2. **"I quit my dream career" confessionals** — the recurring viral unit on TikTok/Substack. Every interview post is one; pitch interviewees to share their own.
3. **"Companies that hire former X" lists** — proven Pinterest/SEO winner in the teacher niche; post #6 + the lead magnet are built for this.
4. **Real-numbers salary transparency** — "SLP to 96K" style titles outperform; the site already does this. Keep putting numbers in titles.
5. **Institutional anger** travels fastest (see Fix SLP's 40k in 2 years) — but it's off-brand for you. Let Fix SLP carry the rage; you be where people land when they're ready to act on it.

## Measurement (keep it simple)
- Weekly: preview runs, purchases, email signups (MailerLite), top pages (Vercel analytics or GA).
- Per-channel attribution: UTM links per channel (`?utm_source=rehabrebels` etc.) — set these up before the podcast episodes air.
- The number that matters at this stage: **preview runs per week**. Everything above exists to move it; conversion to $24 is the tool's job (and its preview quality is already the pitch).

## What NOT to do
- Don't build a TikTok/IG following from scratch — highest effort per unit of return; partner instead.
- Don't pay for ads yet — at $24 AOV the funnel needs organic validation first.
- Don't link-drop in r/slp or any FB group — one ban costs the channel forever.
- Don't chase the head terms The Non-Clinical PT owns — the long-tail is empty and converts better anyway.
