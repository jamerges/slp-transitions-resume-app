## Cohort curriculum (their whole arc, six sessions)

1. Career direction clarity — workbook, values, skills assessment
2. LinkedIn strategy and modern job search
3. Framework for translating clinical expertise into non-clinical language
4. Resume and cover letter training *(Huggins)*
5. Mock interview workshop with live feedback
6. Personalized action plan

Every week of that is discipline-agnostic. Week 3 is precisely where our
sourced SLP salary bands, timelines and guardrails would make the same session
land harder, and week 4 is the part we have already automated.

## What to take

**$700 is the market price for a 10-week cohort.** Our earlier $499–799 estimate
was right; no need to guess further.

**Their $15 role-specific resume bundle is the sharpest product on the list, and
we can beat it.** Theirs is a template plus two real samples, static and
discipline-agnostic. We have twelve published interviews and a pipeline of
twenty-five more — asking interviewees for their actual (redacted) resume would
produce the same artifact, SLP-specific, with the story already attached.
Demand is measured: Jeannie Baranowski's shared resume template drew 274
reactions and 110 comments in the Facebook group.

⚠️ Note their $15 sits **below** our $24. Ours is generated from your own
resume rather than a template, but the pricing page has to make that obvious or
we look expensive next to them.

**Copy the $149 bundle's structure, not just its price.** Artifacts + a live
session + a voucher toward the cohort. That beats selling a bare coaching hour:
it is not pure time-for-money, and it funnels upward by design.

**Their top of funnel is free co-hosted LinkedIn Lives** — with Lauren Curtis,
The Clinician Transition, MedRhythms' CEO, and alumni panels. Cheap, repeatable,
and we could run it tomorrow with the interview roster.

**The Fractional Clinical Consultant Program is the most defensible thing they
have** — matching clinicians to companies for paid project work. It is the
"network access" deliverable, productized. Worth watching.
