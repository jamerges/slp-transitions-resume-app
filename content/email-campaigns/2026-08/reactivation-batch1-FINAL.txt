James here. You signed up a while back, probably for the list of companies that hire SLPs. A lot has changed since then, so here's the quick version.

The companies list is now a searchable directory – 188 companies, filterable by type, each one linking straight to the company's page. app.slptransitions.com/companies

There's a career quiz. Eight questions, takes two minutes, scored against real documented SLP transitions. You get a best-fit path with actual salary ranges and an honest timeline – not a vague "here are your options" but a direct answer. app.slptransitions.com/quiz

There's an open-roles page that refreshes every Monday. Current openings, grouped by path, with licence-gated and senior-only titles filtered out so you're not wading through things that don't apply to you. app.slptransitions.com/jobs

And I'm publishing a lot more: interviews with SLPs who made the move, and guides on the resume, LinkedIn, and whether to leave at all. If you read one thing, make it the 13 non-clinical paths piece. slptransitions.com/alternative-careers-speech-pathologists-slps/

Quick reintro since it's been a while: I was a school and private-practice SLP. I now do content strategy and marketing at a mental-health-tech company. This site is everything I wish I'd had when I was trying to decide whether (and how) to leave.

If you're still doing clinical work and some days feel like too much, you're not broken and you're definitely not alone. Wanting something different isn't a failure of commitment. It's just information. You're allowed to act on it, at whatever pace makes sense.

I'll write roughly weekly from here (but I'm human too so be patient with me as I curate the best resources for you). One useful thing at a time.
