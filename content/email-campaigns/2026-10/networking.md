Hi {$name|default(there)},

Most SLPs who land a non-clinical job get there through a person, not a job board. One SLP sent 113 tailored applications over eleven months for a single offer. The moves that go faster almost always have someone on the inside.

You already know how to talk to strangers. You build rapport all day, with a four-year-old, a nurse and a grandmother before lunch. What's missing is people who work somewhere else, because twelve years in one profession fills your contacts with SLPs.

So here are the two questions I'd save for the last two minutes of any conversation with someone who works where you'd like to:

**"Is there anyone else you'd talk to if you were me?"**

**"If a [role] opens on your team, would you be open to me sending you the posting?"**

The first one hands you the next name, which is how one conversation becomes five. The second is a referral ask in its least awkward form: a yes or no about something that hasn't happened yet.
