## E4 · day 7 · "Managed a caseload of 62" vs. "managed a portfolio of 62 accounts"
**Subject:** Same job. Different words. Only one gets interviews.
**Preview:** Your résumé is being read in the wrong dictionary.

Hi {$name|default(there)},

A bullet from a real school SLP's résumé:

"Manage caseload of 62 students with IEPs across 3 school buildings"

A hiring manager at an ed-tech company reads that in about seven seconds (the real average) and files it: clinical person, wrong pile.

The same experience, translated:

"Managed a portfolio of 62 concurrent client accounts across 3 sites, each with individual goals, timelines, and quarterly progress reviews"

Nothing invented. Same Tuesday afternoon, same IEPs, same data collection, same angry-teacher diplomacy. Now it's in words the hiring manager can map to her posting.

You have the skills. You've been describing them in a language the buyer doesn't speak.

A few more translations from SLPs who got hired:
- IEP meetings → cross-functional stakeholder alignment
- Chart audits → quality assurance
- Talking a skeptical parent into a treatment plan → objection handling (sales and customer-success managers light up at this one)

The full list, with the exact words:
