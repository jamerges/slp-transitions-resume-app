# Lesson briefs, Modules 2–6 (what each lesson must cover; tools available)

## Module 2: Explore (Weeks 2–3). "Twenty real paths with what they pay and how long they take, so you can pick one and stop reading."
- 2.1 The map: twenty paths by timeline. explainer. Tools: `path-map`. Three tiers: out in weeks (license is the credential), 6–12 months (some upskilling), 12–24 months (long builds). Ranges from research-facts. Telehealth is a bridge, not a path.
- 2.2 Out in weeks: four jobs your license already qualifies you for. Liaison, utilization review, clinical educator/trainer at device companies, case management. What the day is (O*NET tasks in research/), who hires (named employers from research-facts), pay, the catch.
- 2.3 Where SLPs actually land. Customer success/implementation, project/program management, healthcare data, content/marketing. Documented outcomes and quotes. Entry doors.
- 2.4 The twelve-month builds. Clinical informatics (Epic sponsorship), instructional design (portfolio), UX (saturation), software (two documented SLPs), conversation design. Honest costs.
- 2.5 Epic, MSL and UX research: the facts the forums get wrong. Guardrails with the alternative route for each.
- 2.6 Pick one: your Pivot Report. Tools: `pivot-report` (links to the included $9 report flow with their path). How to read the report; what "readiness" means.
- 2.7 Path deep-dive (your top two). Tools: `path-deep-dive` (renders postings + employers + entry door for the chosen path). The lesson text teaches how to read three postings side by side.
- 2.8 Messages that get answered. The informational-interview message (one-in-four reply rate from research-facts), what to ask, what to do after. `script` blocks for the message, the follow-up, the thank-you.
- 2.9 Reach out to three people. action. Tools: `contact-tracker`. Badge: first-reach-out.

## Module 3: Translate (Weeks 3–5). "Rewrite your résumé and LinkedIn so a hiring manager can see what you already do."
- 3.1 Seven seconds. That's the whole first read. explainer. The 7.4-second first pass, the two piles, the ATS myth (systems rank, humans reject), the three rejection reasons for career changers.
- 3.2 Caseload, IEP, chart audit: the same words, translated. The translation pairs from research-facts and the transferable-skills post. Tools: `translation-pairs`. Example blocks (before/after bullets).
- 3.3 Numbers you already have. Tools: `number-mining` (a worksheet that pulls real numbers: caseload size, people trained, rollouts, outcomes). "No number, no bullet."
- 3.4 Why your cover letter sounds like the other 34. The 62% stat, the 35% identical answers, the tell-words, how to use AI without the slop.
- 3.5 Build your résumé in the Suite. Tools: `suite-link`. What to paste, what the preview shows, how to refine the six sections. action. Badge: first-translation.
- 3.6 LinkedIn as the background check. Headline, About, experience rewrite, recruiter search terms per path. Tools: `linkedin-checklist`.
- 3.7 The cover letter, and why it only works if it cost you something. Structure, pull not push (as a rule, not a slogan), one paragraph only you could write. `script` skeleton.
- 3.8 Send one application properly. action. The 48-hour rule, tailoring, tracking. Tools: `application-tracker`. Badge: application-1.

## Module 4: Test (Weeks 5–8). "Build one piece of proof, keep your paycheck while you do it, and find the person who'll vouch for you."
- 4.1 "No one took me seriously with a CAPM". Proof over certificates: the CAPM quote, portfolio-over-credential in instructional design, the one-honest-case-study line from UX, Google PM cert as an exception (cheap, digestible).
- 4.2 The proof-artifact menu. Tools: `artifact-menu` (one brief per path). Each brief: what to make, how long, what it proves, where to put it.
- 4.3 Make it in a week. action. Badge: proof-artifact. Tools: `artifact-menu`.
- 4.4 Keeping the paycheck while you leave. Part-time, PRN, telepractice as a bridge, contract work. What each buys and costs. Telehealth is clinical.
- 4.5 The loan is a calendar: PSLF, the pay-cut budget, COBRA. Use research/pslf-and-money.md. Tools: `runway-calculator`. Honest: what qualifies, the 120-payment count, employer certification, the buffer months, COBRA cost ranges, when to let the C's lapse (the 5-year Praxis rule from the corpus).
- 4.6 500 applications, or one person who'll vouch for you. The referral math, the 48-hour posting rule, converting a conversation into a referral. Quotes from research-facts.
- 4.7 Keep two conversations warm. action. Tools: `contact-tracker`.

## Module 5: Leap (Weeks 8–12). "Interviews, the three questions every career changer gets, and how to handle the offer."
- 5.1 How career changers get screened. Retention doubt, jargon they can't map, no proof of investment. The three answers.
- 5.2 Bridge statements. Tools: `bridge-builder` (fills a template from their pull sentence, path, and one number). `script` blocks.
- 5.3 Screening questions that filter you out. The questions, what a good answer contains, examples. Tools: `screening-questions`.
- 5.4 The mock interview. Tools: `mock-interview` (AI coach). action. Badge: interview-1. The text: how to use it, what to listen for in your own answers.
- 5.5 Salary and the offer. Use research/negotiation.md. Anchoring on the documented range, what to do with a lowball, when a cut is acceptable (research coordinator as springboard), total comp, the counter script.
- 5.6 113 applications, 7 interviews, 1 offer. Rejection as a volume statistic; the weekly numbers to track. Tools: `application-tracker`.
- 5.7 The first 90 days. Clinician habits that help (documentation, stakeholder reads), the ones to drop (over-explaining, waiting to be told), how to bank the first win.
- 5.8 Accept, or keep going. action. Badge: offer. Tools: `offer-checklist`.

## Module 6: After. "What to do once you're in, and how to help the next person out."
- 6.1 Tell your story. action. Link the story form (https://slptransitions.com/about/). What to include (stage you started in, setting, years, first month).
- 6.2 A year in: what changes. Ladders exist in non-clinical work (the "only lateral moves for 30 years" quote from the corpus); when to move again; management and leadership paths from the pillar.
- 6.3 Answer one message from someone in Module 2. action. Why replies matter (the one-in-four rate), how to answer well in ten minutes.
