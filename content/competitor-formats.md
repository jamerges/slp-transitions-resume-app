## Specimen 1: the Lauren Braley income-transparency post (verbatim, saved 2026-08-01)

> I made over $100,000 from posting on LinkedIn as a Non-Clinical Career Coach
> No paid ads. / No viral dance videos (although maybe i should start?) / No huge team.
> Just consistently creating content about helping clinicians transition into non-clinical careers.
> For everyone who loves salary transparency, here's a breakdown of exactly where that money has came from:
> 💼 85% → 1:1 asynchronous coaching … Every single coaching client came from inbound LinkedIn messages 🤯 / Ad spend: $0
> 🤝 10% → Affiliate commissions from two products I have personally used…
> 🗺️ 3% → Sales from my digital product, the Non-Clinical Market Map…
> 🎤 2% → Partnerships with other non-clinical career coaches…
> The money didn't come from trying to become a creator.
> It came from solving the same problem over and over, building trust, and showing up consistently.
> My name is Lauren Braley PT, DPT, NCS. I'm a Non-Clinical healthcare career coach who works remotely in health tech as a Client Success Manager, and I help clinicians successfully transition into non-clinical careers on the side 🌱
> if you liked this post come follow along as i ungatekeep how to pivot into non clinical roles successfully

**The skeleton (reusable):**
1. **Number-claim hook** — specific, verifiable-feeling, slightly outrageous
2. **Negation triad** — "No X. No Y. No Z." (kills the obvious objections instantly)
3. **"Just [unsexy method]"** — makes it feel replicable
4. **Receipts block** — emoji + % + source lines; transparency as generosity
5. **Reframe couplet** — "It didn't come from X. It came from Y."
6. **Identity paragraph** — name, credential, day job (credibility: she *did the thing*)
7. **Soft follow CTA** with a persona word ("ungatekeep")

**Why it works for clinicians specifically:** the audience is hyper-skeptical of
coaches (see VoC §3) — transparency-with-receipts is the exact antidote. Note the
imperfect grammar and lowercase; polish would *hurt* it.

**James remixes** (when the numbers exist — never invent):
- "N SLPs took the career quiz this month. Here's exactly what they picked" + % breakdown by path
- "$X from a $9 report: exact breakdown of my first month selling to SLPs" (after a real month)
- "I read N job postings that hired former SLPs. Here's the exact language they use" (company DB → receipts)

## Platform notes

- **LinkedIn** (Lauren's lesson): first 2 lines decide everything (the "see more" fold);
  short lines, white space, no links in body (comment instead); identity paragraph at
  the *bottom* so strangers get context after the value; imperfection reads as human.
- **Instagram** (TCT/TCC): carousels = listicle formats 4/5/9 sliced one-point-per-slide;
  slide 1 is a hook card, last slide is the CTA. James can repurpose blog H2s directly.
- **Blog/SEO** (NonClinicalPT/TCC): role pages + segment pages interlinked; FAQ blocks
  with schema (AEO); every post ends in the quiz, not the product (quiz converts cold
  traffic better).
